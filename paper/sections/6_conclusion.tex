\section{Conclusion}
STRATA-AdamW combines nested gradient subspaces, multi-timescale covariance sketches, and transported adaptive state. The analysis connects flag geometry and covariance tracking to explicit update perturbations, nonconvex stationarity, PL contraction, and full-state stability and PAC--Bayes generalization. Separate moment counters and a projected-statistic epsilon floor resolve the two observed numerical failure modes.

Completed comparisons identify benefits on specific graph, forecasting, recommendation, and continual-learning settings. The completed 180-configuration extension favors STRATA over same-rank Single-Grassmann in five of six settings; ETTh2 achieves the lowest MSE among all five tested methods, and both MovieLens datasets improve on Single-Grassmann. PubMed/SAGE favors the full design over all ten tested interventions; its equal-state, equal-count search retains a 0.460-percentage-point advantage over Single-Grassmann. Real sensor-drift replay provides an accuracy--state tradeoff, reaching 96.506\% with 55.1\% of AdamW's stored optimizer state. The complete single-seed comparison matrices and validation-based selection records give the empirical context for these results and the conditional theoretical guarantees.
