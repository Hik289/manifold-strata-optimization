\begin{abstract}
STRATA-AdamW stores adaptive optimizer state on a nested flag manifold, using multi-timescale covariance sketches, transported first moments, and blockwise adaptive updates. Separate moment counters and a projected-statistic epsilon floor make the resulting update numerically stable. Completed seed-42 comparisons show gains over same-rank Single-Grassmann in three of twelve citation-graph settings and five of six newly tested forecasting, recommendation, and sensor-drift settings. On ETTh2, STRATA lowers MSE by 19.6\% relative to AdamW and 2.7\% relative to GaLore; sensor-drift replay reaches 96.506\% accuracy using 55.1\% of AdamW's stored state, versus AdamW's 96.536\%. On PubMed/SAGE, the full method exceeds all ten tested interventions, while a state-matched six-candidate comparison gives a smaller 0.460-percentage-point gain over Single-Grassmann. We develop detailed conditional guarantees for flag optimality, statistical tracking, adaptive-state perturbations, nonconvex and PL convergence, and generalization through coupled optimizer states and an inverse-KL, flag-aligned PAC--Bayes bound. Complete comparisons retain stronger baselines and identify the settings in which the combined design is effective.
\end{abstract}
